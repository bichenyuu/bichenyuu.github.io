**August 4, 2026 · Boston, MA**

I presented my poster, **“A Two-Stage Modeling Approach to Risk Prediction with High-Dimensional Two-Phase Data,”** at the Biometrics Section’s contributed poster session during the **2026 Joint Statistical Meetings (JSM)**.

It was my first time attending such a large academic conference, and I was truly excited to be part of it. From inspiring talks to engaging discussions, I learned so much and came away with new ideas and wonderful memories.

It was also my first visit to Boston, and I quickly fell in love with the charm of this beautiful city. Sitting by the **Charles River** at sunset, watching the golden light shimmer across the water, runners passing by along the riverbank, and the beautiful melodies drifting from the distant **Hatch Shell**—everything felt so peaceful and magical.

A journey filled with learning, inspiration, and moments I wish could last a little longer.
